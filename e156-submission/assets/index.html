RMST Meta: A Browser Tool for Meta-Analysis of Restricted Mean Survival Time Beyond the Hazard Ratio

Mahmood Ahmad · Methods · 2026-03-26

RMST Meta pools restricted mean survival time differences across trials in the browser, finding immunotherapy extended mean survival by 2.59 months (95% CI 1.94-3.23) in five NSCLC PD-1 inhibitor trials.
